A front desk that never sleeps.

Using PayMeGPT, we train an assistant on your services, hours, prices, and policies. It answers the questions your team hears every day, asks the right follow-ups to spot serious buyers, and drops booked appointments right into your calendar.

Wallet Pass Loyalty

The loyalty card that lives on their phone.

Paper punch cards get lost. Apps don't get downloaded. Wallet passes sit right next to your customers' boarding passes and payment cards in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet, added in a single tap from a link, QR code, or text.
